# A short Tabletop demo

Use one native Expo Go host and one or two phone-browser guests. Keep the host's Metro tunnel open for Expo Go. The HTTPS guest site works independently of that tunnel.

1. **Start together.** Open Tabletop on the host phone, tap Start a table, enter a name, and create the table. Have friends use the displayed QR with their normal camera, or share the invite. They enter names without email registration. Point out that everybody now has a persistent seat at the same table.
2. **Solve together.** Start The Daily Mini. Fill one Across entry on each of two devices and show the letters updating. For November 15, the five Across answers are **GAMES, ALERT, METRO, ERROR, STORE**. Finish the grid, show the shared time, and copy the result. The very short times in automated evidence are not human solve times.
3. **Keep the table.** Choose another game, select Hanabi, and start. Show your own question-mark cards and a teammate's visible faces. Open the tutorial from help if the audience is new to the rules.
4. **Make a firework.** Give the next player a number-1 clue if their visible hand contains a 1. With all piles at zero, they can safely play a marked 1. Show the score/deck change and the shared card/firework effect. A mistaken out-of-sequence play has a different fuse-loss effect; never claim a deliberately risky play was a known safe play.
5. **Show Property Rush.** For a short demo, the host can use help → End game and choose another, then explicitly confirm ending without a result. Start Property Rush in the same lobby. Bank cards, place a property, and use Rent when available. Show the target's Accept/Block response and bank-only payment. With a bank card worth 3, paying rent of 2 transfers the whole card with no change. Deals are genuinely shuffled, so do not promise a Rent card in the opening hand.
6. **Show persistence.** Background one phone and reopen it. Its name, seat, game, and any pending response remain. Refresh a browser guest to demonstrate the same behavior. Do not clear browser storage or reinstall Expo Go during this demonstration.

7. **Ask a rule, optionally.** During a game, use the chat bubble immediately left of the question mark. A host can also open it from the selected game's lobby rules. Select or type a question and explicitly tap Ask Keiki or the keyboard's Send. For Property Rush, ask whether paying 3 for rent of 2 gives change. Rules questions do not attach the live table. Do not type private hands or personal information.
8. **Get a second opinion, optionally.** During your Property Rush turn, open the chat bubble and read the advisor's disclosure: tapping Suggest a move sends your hand and public board to Keiki, which stores requests. Ask once and show that no move happens. Choose Use this move, then Keep thinking to cancel without changing the table. Reopen the confirmation and tap Confirm move to play through the normal rules. If anyone changes the table first, the old advice expires. Hanabi and Daily Mini have no live advisor.

Tutorials contain imaginary practice hands and never modify the table. Keiki explanations are AI-generated; the authoritative rules and tutorials remain usable if the helper is unavailable. Physical-device verification must be recorded separately from Revyl's cloud-device evidence.
